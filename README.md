# Inventory-Management
# 📦 Inventory Management Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to maintain optimal inventory levels. Excess inventory increases carrying costs and leads to dead stock, while insufficient inventory causes stockouts, delayed deliveries, and lost sales opportunities. Without effective inventory classification and replenishment planning, businesses face inefficient working capital utilization and reduced service levels.

This project aims to optimize inventory performance by analyzing SKU contribution, demand variability, stock status, and replenishment requirements using ABC-XYZ analysis and inventory turnover metrics.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Identify high-value products contributing most to revenue.
- Classify inventory based on demand variability.
- Monitor stock levels and identify understock and overstock situations.
- Improve inventory turnover and reduce carrying costs.
- Detect products requiring replenishment.
- Support data-driven procurement and inventory planning.

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Inventory Concentration
- Category A products contribute the majority of inventory value despite representing a smaller percentage of total SKUs.
- Category C products account for many SKUs but contribute relatively less to overall revenue.

### 2. Demand Variability
- X-category items exhibit stable demand and are ideal candidates for automated replenishment.
- Z-category products show unpredictable demand and require higher safety stock or periodic review.

### 3. Inventory Efficiency
- Certain ABC-XYZ combinations demonstrate low inventory turnover, indicating excess stock and inefficient capital utilization.
- Fast-moving products generate higher turnover and should receive priority during replenishment.

### 4. Stock Imbalance
- Understocked products increase the risk of lost sales and customer dissatisfaction.
- Overstocked products tie up capital and increase storage costs.

### 5. Replenishment Requirements
- Multiple SKUs have crossed reorder points and require immediate procurement action to avoid stockouts.

### 6. Sales Trend Patterns
- Weekly sales trends reveal demand fluctuations, enabling better forecasting and inventory planning.

# 💡 Recommendations

### ✅ Prioritize High-Value Products
- Focus inventory monitoring on A-category items since they contribute the highest business value.
- Maintain higher service levels for these products to prevent revenue loss.

### ✅ Optimize Safety Stock Levels
- Maintain lower safety stock for stable demand (X-category) products.
- Increase safety stock for highly variable (Z-category) items to minimize stockout risk.

### ✅ Reduce Dead and Excess Inventory
- Identify slow-moving products with low turnover ratios.
- Implement discount campaigns or liquidation strategies to free up working capital.

### ✅ Improve Replenishment Planning
- Automate replenishment for fast-moving products.
- Continuously monitor products approaching reorder points.

### ✅ Increase Inventory Turnover
- Align purchasing decisions with actual demand patterns.
- Reduce overstocked inventory and improve stock rotation.

### ✅ Forecast Demand More Accurately
- Use historical weekly sales trends for demand forecasting.
- Incorporate seasonality and demand variability into procurement decisions.

### ✅ Focus on ABC-XYZ Segments
- **AX Products:** High value and stable demand → Highest priority.
- **AZ Products:** High value but unpredictable demand → Require close monitoring.
- **CX Products:** Low value and stable demand → Simple replenishment policies.
- **CZ Products:** Low value and erratic demand → Consider minimizing inventory levels.
